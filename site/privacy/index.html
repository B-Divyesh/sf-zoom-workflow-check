Privacy policy

Privacy

Effective 6 September 2026

Summary

The CLI has no account, analytics, telemetry, advertising, or cloud report service. Workflow files, page details, and screenshots are written only to the output path you choose.

Documentation site

This static documentation site does not load third-party scripts or analytics. Its service worker stores public pages and illustration files in your browser cache so the guide can reload offline after the first visit. Clear this site’s stored data in your browser to remove that cache.

CLI evidence

A report can contain URLs, accessible names, layout measurements, and screenshots from the pages you test. Those files may be sensitive even though the tool does not upload them. Keep them in controlled CI artifacts, review them before sharing, and delete them under your project’s retention policy.

Network requests

Chromium visits the workflow URL you provide and makes the requests that page normally makes. Zoom Workflow Check does not add tracking or contact a Zoom Workflow Check server.

Questions

Open an issue in the source repository without private screenshots or customer data.
